### 📜 Full Judgment / Award PDF Text

```text
IN THE COURT OF THE SUBORDINATE JUDGE AT AVINASHI
PRESENT: 3-Subordinate Judge, Avinashi
Saturday, the 14th day of July 2018
Original Suit No. 126 / 2018 (CNR: TNTI160003232018)

BETWEEN:
1) Karunayathal
2) Kannammal
3) P. Shanmugam
4) Thangaraj
All residing at Avinashi, Tirunelveli District.           ... Plaintiffs / Petitioners

AND:
1) Santhamani
Residing at Avinashi, Tirunelveli District.               ... Defendant / Respondent

Suit filed under Order VII Rule 1 & Section 7(1) of the Code of Civil Procedure.

LOK ADALAT AWARD & FINAL JUDGMENT:
This suit coming on this 14th day of July 2018 for final hearing before the Lok Adalat Bench 
held at Sub Court, Avinashi, in the presence of Plaintiffs 1 to 4 and their counsel 
Mr. P. Karthikeyan, Advocate, and Defendant Santhamani.

Upon hearing both parties and perusing the joint memorandum of compromise filed by the 
parties, the Lok Adalat Bench is satisfied that the compromise reached is voluntary, 
genuine, and lawful.

IT IS ORDERED AND DECREED AS FOLLOWS:
1. The suit is hereby decreed in terms of the Joint Compromise Petition dated 14-07-2018.
2. The Joint Compromise Petition shall form an integral part of this Decree and Judgment.
3. In accordance with Section 21 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, full court fees 
   paid on the plaint shall be refunded to the Plaintiffs.
4. Each party shall bear their own costs.

Given under my hand and the seal of the Court this 14th day of July 2018.

Sd/- 
Subordinate Judge, Avinashi (Seal)
```
